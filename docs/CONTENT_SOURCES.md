# Demo content references

The Merdeka demo distinguishes independence on 31 August 1957 from the formation of Malaysia on 16 September 1963. Its 1955 election and Stadium Merdeka facts were checked against [Arkib Negara: Menjejaki 31 Ogos 1957](https://pustakailmu.arkib.gov.my/index.php/ms/pustaka-ilmu/jendela-sejarah/menjejaki-31-ogos-1957). Formation date: [Government of Malaysia: Malaysian history](https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/government/learn-about-malaysia/malaysian-history).

The illustrated heart and timeline map are schematic educational diagrams, not medical or geographic measurements. Generated provider content still needs educational review before broad school deployment.
